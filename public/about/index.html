About

We are a small studio. We take on a handful of projects a year and finish every one of them properly.

Proiectum was founded in 2021 and works across identity, motion and interface. We are deliberately small — the people you meet are the people who do the work.

We prefer long engagements to quick ones, and we would rather ship one considered thing than five hurried ones.
